GOOD TO KNOW

Before you order.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes, pan-India shipping is available for packaged pickles. Charges depend on destination and weight.

Can I pay on delivery?

No. All orders are prepaid. Cash on Delivery (COD) is not available.

When is your kitchen open?

Evening service is 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM. Thursday is our kitchen rest and preparation day.

How can I place an order?

Add items to your bag and send your order details through WhatsApp. Our team will confirm availability, shipping and payment instructions.

Can I cancel an order?

Orders cannot be cancelled after preparation begins. Please confirm your details before placing an order.
